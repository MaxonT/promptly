Understanding Token Credits

🎯

What are Tokens?

Tokens are units used by AI models to process text. Each word typically uses 1-4 tokens. Your usage is metered based on both input and output tokens.

📊

Transparent Billing

After each run, you'll see a detailed breakdown: input tokens, output tokens, total tokens used, and your remaining balance. No hidden fees.

🔄

Daily Refresh

Get free daily tokens that refresh at UTC midnight. These are used first before your monthly allocation, maximizing your value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

What happens after my trial?

After your 14-day trial ends, you can subscribe to continue using Promptly. Your unused tokens will expire, but you won't be charged automatically.

Do unused tokens roll over?

Monthly tokens do not roll over to the next period. Daily free tokens can accumulate up to 2 days' worth (40K max).

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through Stripe's secure payment platform. We never store your card details.
