📖 Metrics Glossary

Total Users

Formula: COUNT(*) FROM analytics_users

The cumulative count of all registered users on the platform since launch (2024-11-29). This includes both active and inactive accounts.

DAU (Daily Active Users)

Formula: COUNT(DISTINCT user_id) FROM analytics_sessions WHERE date(session_start) = [target_date]

The number of unique users who initiated at least one session on a specific calendar day. A user is counted only once regardless of how many sessions they start.

WAU (Weekly Active Users)

Formula: COUNT(DISTINCT user_id) FROM analytics_sessions WHERE date(session_start) > date([reference_date], '-7 days')

The number of unique users who initiated at least one session within the past 7 days. Each user is counted only once even if they were active on multiple days.

MAU (Monthly Active Users)

Formula: COUNT(DISTINCT user_id) FROM analytics_sessions WHERE date(session_start) > date([reference_date], '-30 days')

The number of unique users who initiated at least one session within the past 30 days. Each user is counted only once regardless of activity frequency.

Stickiness (DAU/MAU Ratio)

Formula: (DAU / MAU) × 100%

Measures user retention and engagement. Higher values indicate users return frequently. Industry benchmark: 20-25% is good, >50% is excellent. Our current metric reflects a "struggling product" phase.

Bounce Rate

Formula: AVG(bounce_rate) FROM analytics_daily WHERE date > [reference_date - 7 days]

The percentage of sessions where users leave after viewing only one page. Calculated as a 7-day rolling average. Lower is better; >40% typically indicates UX issues.

New Users (Last 24h)

Formula: COUNT(*) FROM analytics_users WHERE created_at > datetime([latest_user_time], '-1 day')

Users who registered within the last 24 hours relative to the most recent registration timestamp in the database.

Sessions

Source: analytics_sessions table

A session begins when a user opens the app and ends after 30 minutes of inactivity or when explicitly closed. Each session tracks page views, duration, and user behavior.

Engagement Metrics

Formula: AVG([metric]) FROM analytics_behavior WHERE recorded_at > [reference_date - 7 days]

Mouse movements, scrolls, clicks, and typing events are tracked per session and averaged over the last 7 days to indicate user interaction depth.

⚠️ Data Notes

Historical Data (2024-11-29 to 2025-01-30): Early-stage product metrics reflecting initial market validation period with organic user acquisition.

Live Data (2025-01-31 onwards): Real-time tracking of user sessions and interactions as the product evolves.

Reference Date: All relative calculations (e.g., "last 7 days") use the most recent date with recorded activity, ensuring accurate historical analysis.
